X23D · Real-time intelligence for surgical guidance

From intraoperative data into real-time surgical intelligence

X23D develops AI software for surgical navigation and robotics, transforming intraoperative imaging already available in the operating room into workflow-native, real-time, patient-specific 3D anatomical insight. By unlocking the latent value of existing imaging systems—without adding complex hardware or disrupting established workflows—we deliver spatial understanding of the surgical site and enable accessible, intelligent, scalable surgical guidance.

AI-powered surgical guidance

Sparse intraoperative imaging turned into context-aware 3D guidance.

X23D-Spine is AI-powered surgical navigation software that transforms a few routine intraoperative X-rays into patient-specific 3D anatomy, giving surgical teams spatial guidance within the existing imaging workflow.

Clinical validation · from day zero

Designed with surgeons.
Tested in the workflow.

Clinical validation is not a final checkpoint. It begins before the first line of code and continues through development, deployment and post-market learning.

Working side by side with orthopaedic surgeons, we evaluate algorithms, interfaces and complete navigation workflows in high-quality surgical facilities. Every cycle turns clinical insight into product requirements, so accuracy, usability and workflow fit are engineered in from the ground up.
